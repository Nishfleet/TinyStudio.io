Proof-safe promise

This is not a cheap Meta ads agency and it does not guarantee ROAS, revenue, or booked calls.

The sprint installs the agency setup brain: offer diagnosis, funnel choice, lead handling, CRM discipline, and reporting. Ad spend changes stay approval-gated.

The real bottleneck

Most high-ticket teams do not need more ads first.

They need a working lead-to-call system: a clear offer, the right funnel, qualified leads, fast follow-up, reminders, CRM tracking, and a decision loop that shows what is broken.

What gets installed

A guided operating system for high-ticket pipeline creation.

Each module produces a usable asset or checklist. The first version is guided by a TinyStudio operator; deeper self-serve automation comes after 3-5 real customer sprints.

Budget

Creative

4 first creatives

Start with two audiences by two pain points. Use raw founder video, plain Canva, or simple captioned video before polishing.

Hook

Script is targeting

First 5 seconds: audience callout, problem, pain, proof, CTA. Iterate hooks first while keeping the body mostly stable.

Funnel

WhatsApp or lead form first

For India, validate with WhatsApp message ads or Meta lead forms before a landing page. Add profile traffic when founder authority matters.
