# TinyStudio — The Website Appraisal and The Growth Desk

TinyStudio sells one human-reviewed managed service around the page a business's revenue depends on: The Website Appraisal, a free leak audit of high-ticket service homepages, and The Growth Desk that closes what the audit finds. It is a managed service with human review, not autonomous software.

## The Website Appraisal

The Website Appraisal is a complimentary leak audit, yours to keep whether or not TinyStudio is ever engaged. It serves high-ticket service businesses — clinics, surgeons, dentists, spas, property, dealers, brokers and similar — and clients are never named.

- Reads the one page a business's revenue depends on, the way a customer with intent reads it
- Four passes: the money page, the paid creative, the unanswered pre-purchase questions, the reach
- Each pass run by a specialist with one job; the result read and signed by a person before it is delivered
- Delivers a short, plain document: each fault named, in the order it costs the business, with the fix beside it
- Findings inside five working days; no call at any point
- Six audits a month; when the sixth is taken, the intake closes until the next

## The Growth Desk

- $2,500 a month on a three-month minimum
- Month one corrects the costliest fault: the page rewritten or rebuilt, a dev-ready handoff if the client's own team ships it, and search-trust basics
- Months two and three build the loop that keeps the standard up: weekly checks, one revision, and tracking that says whether the fix held
- One promise about outcomes, and only one: the delivery guarantee. If the month-one deliverables are not in the client's hands within fourteen working days of Day 0, month one is refunded in full. Day 0 begins only once payment, access, the approval owner and the implementation owner are recorded; waiting on the client pauses the clock

## How The Work Runs

Seven specialist agents do the research, drafting and checking, each with a single job, a fixed set of inputs and a checklist it must satisfy, working from the client's own pages and numbers — never a template for the sector. A person reviews fit, every claim, all client-facing work, delivery and renewal, and signs every audit.

Human review gates fit, claims, client-facing work, delivery/acceptance, and renewal. Automation may prepare research, drafts, QA, packages, and routing, but never autonomously sends, publishes, spends, approves, accepts, or renews.

## Evidence

The site's finding is backed by market research that TinyStudio shares freely: an automated read of served homepage HTML, refreshed daily. The current set covers 99 sites across thirteen industries and eight geographies; 89 are readable and ten are blocked by bot protection and excluded from every count. Of the 89 readable, 53 answer none of the questions a buyer asks before committing, and 57 show no price. Inner pages are deliberately not counted. This number is today's and changes as the set is re-read.

## Not Promised

There are no revenue, ranking, ROAS, conversion, booked-call, or sales-volume guarantees. The delivery guarantee is a promise about the work, not the market. Client work is never published: no logos, no case studies, no testimonials, no "as seen at".

## Data Handling

The public app stores email signup and lightweight usage metadata in Cloudflare D1, including daily rate-limit counters and a daily IP-derived rate-limit key. Submitted business context is processed to generate output and is not stored by this app. There is no public endpoint for reading collected emails or usage metadata. Client-side code does not call model providers, platform admin APIs, ad accounts, databases, or private credentials directly.
