### India High-Ticket Test Plan Generator

Pipeline Sprint can draft an India-first validation plan for high-ticket offers: INR 500-INR 1,000/day for 7 or 15 days, four first creatives, hook matrix, WhatsApp vs lead form vs profile traffic recommendation, qualification questions, setter script, reminder/no-show sequence, metric tracker, and decision rules.

Creative rule: script is targeting. The first 5 seconds should call out the exact audience and pain. Start with four simple creatives: two audiences by two pain points. Iterate hooks first and keep the body mostly stable until a pain point is validated.

Funnel rule: for India, test WhatsApp message ads and Meta lead forms before landing pages when the goal is early message validation. Landing pages come later after the offer and message have clearer signal.

## Workflow

The sprint is a four-stage operating loop:

1. Intake: gather the website, priority page, reviews, customer messages, competitors, analytics screenshots, and the buyer action to improve.
2. Production system: prepare the client brain, leak map, rewrite options, search-trust review, starter assets, and handoff package.
3. Human review: check claims, proof, buyer logic, legal-sensitive copy, and whether the recommendation is worth shipping.
4. Handoff loop: deliver the changed asset, proof source, Loom walkthrough, measurement contract, and next 30-day action loop.

The system can prepare work, but it does not auto-publish client assets, auto-send client messages, approve proof claims, change ad budgets, or turn owned-product proof into paid-client proof.
