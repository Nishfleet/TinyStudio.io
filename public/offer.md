# TinyStudio.io — The Leak Audit and The Growth Desk

TinyStudio.io sells one human-reviewed service around the page a business's revenue depends on: a free leak audit as the entry point, and the Growth Desk that closes the leaks the audit finds.

## The Leak Audit (free)

- Reads one page — the one a business's revenue depends on — the way a customer with intent reads it
- Four passes: the money page, the paid creative, the unanswered pre-purchase questions, the reach
- Each pass run by a specialist with one job; the result read and signed by a person before it is delivered
- Delivers a short, plain document: named leaks in the order they cost the business money, with the fix beside each
- Findings inside five working days; no call at any point
- Six audits a month; when the sixth is taken, the intake closes until the next
- Complimentary and yours to keep, whether or not TinyStudio is ever engaged

## The Growth Desk

- $2,500 a month on a three-month minimum
- Month one: the highest-leverage page rewritten or rebuilt, a dev-ready handoff if the client's team ships it, and search-trust basics
- Months two and three: weekly checks, one revision, and tracking that says whether the fix held
- One promise about outcomes, and only one: if the month-one deliverables are not in hand within fourteen working days of Day 0, month one is refunded in full. Day 0 begins once payment, access, the approval owner and the implementation owner are recorded; waiting on the client pauses the clock

## How The Work Runs

Seven specialist agents do research, drafting, quality checks, packaging and routing, each with one job, fixed inputs and a checklist, reading the client's real pages, reviews, ads and numbers. A person reviews fit, every claim, client-facing work, delivery and renewal. Automation never sends, publishes, spends, or approves — nothing leaves the desk without a human signature.

## Not Promised

No revenue, ranking, ROAS, conversion, booked-call or sales-volume guarantees, and no claim about what an open leak costs. No client work is ever published: no logos, case studies or testimonials. Market research (the 89-site study) is shared freely; nothing belonging to a paying client is shared.

## Data Handling

Signup forms collect a business website and an email address, stored in Cloudflare D1 with metadata. No public endpoint reads collected signups. Submitted business context is processed for output and not stored.

## Legacy

The earlier self-serve Agent Desk (one-shot Pipeline Brief) is demoted and not the current offer; its safety rails stand: no campaign publishing, no ad spend changes, no ad account connection, no prospect message sending.
