# TinyStudio Offer

TinyStudio runs a human-reviewed leak audit for high-ticket service businesses, and a desk that closes the leaks the audit finds. The audit is free and the findings are yours to keep.

## The Free Leak Audit

- Four passes, done by hand, on the surfaces that decide whether a ready-to-buy visitor stays that way: the money page, the paid creative, the unanswered pre-purchase questions, and the reach
- A short, plain document in return: named leaks in the order they cost you money, with the fix beside each one
- Findings within five working days. Six audits a month; when the sixth is taken, intake closes until the next month
- Seven specialist agents do the reading, each with a single job, fixed inputs and a checklist; one human reads and signs everything client-facing
- Client work is never published: no logos, no case studies, no testimonials

## The Growth Desk

$2,500 per month on a three-month minimum. Month one closes the leak: the highest-leverage page rewritten or rebuilt, plus a dev-ready handoff if your team ships it. Months two and three: weekly checks, one revision, and tracking that says whether the fix held.

Delivery guarantee: if the month-one deliverables are not in your hands within fourteen working days of Day 0, month one is refunded in full. Day 0 begins only once payment, access and both named owners are recorded; the clock pauses while we wait on you.

## What TinyStudio Does Not Do

- No revenue, ROAS, conversion, booking, ranking or sales-lift guarantees — the delivery guarantee above is the only promise about outcomes
- No campaign publishing
- No ad spend changes
- No ad account connection
- No prospect message sending
- Nothing published to your site
- No storage of submitted business snapshots, optional details, weekly metrics, or generated artifacts
- No replacement for a sales team

## The Agent Desk Behind It

The free brief and the paid desk are powered by the Agent Desk at /agent-desk: seven specialist agents with fixed inputs and checklists, running on Cloudflare Workers AI, generating the Pipeline Brief, Implementation Checklist, and Weekly Fix Report server-side. Client-side code does not call model providers, platform admin APIs, ad accounts, databases, or private credentials directly. A person reviews fit, every claim, all client-facing work, delivery and renewal; automation never sends, publishes, spends, or approves.
