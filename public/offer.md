# TinyStudio — The Leak Audit And Growth Desk

TinyStudio sells a human-reviewed leak audit and Growth Desk for high-ticket businesses.

## The Audit

The audit is free. TinyStudio reads the one page a business's revenue depends on the way a customer with intent reads it, and shows the exact points at which that customer leaves. Four passes, by hand: the money page, the paid creative, the unanswered questions, and the reach. The deliverable is a short, plain document — named leaks in the order they cost the business money, with the fix beside each one. No deck, no dashboard, no jargon.

Intake takes about thirty seconds: a website and an email. No call is required at any point. Six a month; when the sixth is taken, the intake closes until the next month. Findings are delivered inside five working days and are yours to keep, whether or not we ever speak again.

## The Growth Desk

If you want it closed rather than merely found, the desk runs at $2,500 per month on a three-month minimum. Month one closes the leak: the highest-leverage page rewritten or rebuilt, a dev-ready handoff if your team ships it, and search-trust basics — the questions people ask before they commit, answered on the page. Months two and three build the loop: weekly checks, one revision, and tracking that tells you whether the fix held.

Delivery guarantee: if the month-one deliverables are not in your hands within fourteen working days of Day 0, month one is refunded in full. Day 0 begins only once payment, access, the approval owner, and the implementation owner are all recorded. If we are waiting on you, the clock pauses. This is the only promise made about outcomes, and it is a promise about the work, not your market.

## Who Does The Work

Seven specialists, each with a single job, a fixed set of inputs, and a checklist it must satisfy: Landing Page Fixer, Product Page Fixer, Site Architecture Fixer, Ad Angle Generator, Competitor Watcher, Email & SMS Generator, and Weekly Performance Analyst. They read your actual pages, reviews, ads, and numbers, and the competitors around you; they flag every claim that needs proof before it can be said; they run on the current frontier model tier, chosen per job. A person reviews fit, every claim, all client-facing work, delivery, and renewal. Every client-facing output carries one human signature.

## Evidence

The market finding is backed by a continuously refreshed study: an automated read of the served homepage HTML, refreshed daily, of public high-ticket homepages. As of the latest snapshot, 99 sites were attempted across 13 industries and 8 geographies; 89 were readable and 10 were blocked by bot protection and excluded from every count. 53 of the readable 89 answer none of the questions a buyer asks before committing, and 57 show no price. The AI-search reach pass publishes its evidence on the audit page — exact prompts, verbatim answers, cited pages — and it is a record of one day on one set of engines, not a promise of ranking, leads, or visibility. Client work is never published: no logos, no case studies, no testimonials. Market research is shared freely.

## Data Handling

The public app stores email signup and lightweight usage metadata in Cloudflare D1: email, normalized website, source, page path, referer, and user agent, plus daily rate-limit counters and a daily hashed IP-derived rate-limit key. The business snapshot, optional details, weekly metrics, and generated artifacts submitted to the self-serve drafting desk are processed to generate the output and are not stored by this app. Client-side code does not call model providers, platform admin APIs, ad accounts, databases, or private credentials directly; generation runs server-side through Cloudflare Workers AI. There is no public endpoint for reading collected emails or usage metadata.

The self-serve drafting desk at /agent-desk drafts planning assets only; it is not the current public product.
