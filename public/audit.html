The AI-search check

The surfaces people now consult before they commit — ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google's AI answers and the rest — answer from your page whether it gives them anything to answer from. We ask the way a customer asks: named questions, one surface at a time, with the date and the quote recorded. Then a person reads the record, like every other pass. Every record resolves to one of four states.

FoundThe surface answered with your facts, and cited your page.
WrongIt answered about you with stale or invented facts. The quote is in the record.
AbsentIt answered a question you are the obvious answer to, and you did not appear.
Not testedWe did not run this surface. Not tested is not "invisible" — it means the record honestly stops where we did.

It is not a claim about any business, and it is not a promise that anything can be made visible; AI-search answers change day to day, which is why every record carries the date, the question, the quote and the source it came from. Where a record carries a fix, the fix is a page change the captured evidence supports — nothing more.
