## Never on the profile

- Client names, logos, case studies, testimonials, "as seen at", or any
  client work.
- A base city or office address.
- A phone number, founding year, or hourly rate that the live site does
  not state.
- Revenue, ranking, ROAS, conversion, booked-call, or sales-volume
  results or guarantees — including words like "guaranteed growth" or
  "rankings".
- An invented employee count, project size, or award.
- The retired self-serve Agent Desk as the current product (it is legacy,
  not the current offer; llms.txt "Legacy Self-Serve Agent Desk").
- A paid plan, sponsor placement, or Pro upgrade of any kind.

## Manual submission runbook (Nish, human only)

1. Open `https://www.goodfirms.co/get-listed` in a normal browser session.
2. Select the Free plan (`$0 / forever`, `No card required`). Do not
   select a paid plan or sponsor placement.
3. Sign up / sign in with the account GoodFirms accepts. The official
   policy is a manual signup form; do not automate signup.
4. Fill the fields from the table above, verbatim where the form accepts
   the text. Service Categories "can't be edited later" — choose the
   single category that fits without overclaiming; if none fits, stop
   and report.
5. Review against the "Never on the profile" list before submitting.
6. Submit and expect the official flow: "Submit the form and await
   approval" with a "4-Step Research Verification by the Goodfirms
   research team" and an approval window of 2-3 business days.
7. Capture the receipt: the real GoodFirms profile URL once published,
   the pending-review state, or the rejection response. Record it in the
   receipt block below and in the growth-loop packet.

No agent creates the account, fills the form, or submits. No unattended
submission. No scraping, no crawling, no bots. GoodFirms terms of use
explicitly bar "software, devices, robots, scripts, or other means to
scrape, crawl, or index any web page or other portion of our service",
and that bar is observed here.

## Reject conditions (stop, do not submit, report)

Per the packet "reject if" clause and its policy line:

- GoodFirms requires a paid plan before a free profile is possible.
- GoodFirms requests proof this business cannot supply (for example, a
  named city, phone, founding year, employee count we cannot state
  truthfully, or named client references).
- The live offer cannot be represented without inventing a location,
  phone, founding year, clients, outcomes, an hourly rate, or a project
  size.
- No service category in GoodFirms' taxonomy fits the offer without
  overclaiming (remember: "can't be edited later").
- The `4-Step Research Verification by the Goodfirms research team`
  returns a rejection.

In any of these cases the truthful action is to record the blocker in
this receipt block and the growth-loop packet, and stop — silence or
invention is not an outcome.
