## Why this handoff exists

The research-desk run on 2026-08-09 verified Clutch's official listing guidance
(`https://help.clutch.co/en/knowledge/get-listed-on-clutch`): a free company profile
is created at `https://clutch.co/get-listed`, includes company name, tagline, website
URL, overview, and service focus, and "Once you have submitted your profile, it will
be sent to our team for review and publishing." A current web search for
`site:clutch.co tinystudio.io TinyStudio` returned no TinyStudio/tinystudio.io
profile — unrelated Tiny Studio entities and generic category pages only. That search
is a baseline, not proof of non-existence. The backlog impact: qualified buyers on a
relevant service-discovery surface have no truthful TinyStudio profile to evaluate.

Automation disposition (from the packet): ambiguous / manual-only. The official page
documents an authenticated submission and review process but does not explicitly
permit unattended automation. Absence of permission is not permission: no bot, no
scraping, no account creation by any agent, no unattended submission.

## Never on the profile

- Client names, logos, case studies, testimonials, "as seen at", or any client work.
- A base city or office address.
- Revenue, ranking, ROAS, conversion, booked-call, or sales-volume results or
  guarantees — including words like "guaranteed growth" or "rankings".
- An invented hourly rate, project size, employee count, or award.
- The retired self-serve Agent Desk as the current product (it is legacy, not the
  current offer; llms.txt "Legacy Self-Serve Agent Desk").

## Manual submission runbook (Nish, human only)

1. Open `https://clutch.co/get-listed` in a normal browser session.
2. Select the Basic (free) profile offering. Do not select Verified or Advertiser.
3. Sign in with a LinkedIn, Google, or company email account (the official options).
4. Fill the fields from the table above, verbatim where the form accepts the text.
5. Review against the "Never on the profile" list before submitting.
6. Submit and expect the official flow: "Once you have submitted your profile, it
   will be sent to our team for review and publishing."
7. Capture the receipt: the real Clutch profile URL once published, or the rejection
   response if review fails. Record it in the receipt block below and in the
   growth-loop packet.

No agent creates the account, fills the form, or submits. No unattended submission.

## Reject conditions (stop, do not submit, report)

Per the packet "reject if" clause and its policy line:

- Clutch requires paid placement before a Basic profile is possible.
- Clutch requests proof this business cannot supply (for example, named client
  references, an office address, or a team size we cannot state truthfully).
- The live offer cannot be represented without inventing location, clients,
  outcomes, an hourly rate, or a project size.
- No service category in Clutch's taxonomy fits the offer without overclaiming.

In any of these cases the truthful action is to record the blocker in this receipt
block and the growth-loop packet, and stop — silence or invention is not an outcome.
