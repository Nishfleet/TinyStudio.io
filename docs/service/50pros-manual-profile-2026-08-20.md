## Why this handoff exists

The research-desk run on 2026-08-20 identified 50Pros — a B2B agency
directory with 100+ service categories — as a live buyer-discovery surface
where TinyStudio is absent. The conversion-audit category
(`https://www.50pros.com/hire/conversion-rate-optimization/conversion-audit`)
lists 35 agencies (Jul 2026) and its own copy prices the service norm at
"$2,500 to $15,000", which makes TinyStudio's free human-reviewed appraisal
a price-and-proof wedge. This lane re-verified the official venue guidance
live on 2026-08-20 in a real browser session (the pages block scripted
fetches — see "What this document does not claim"):

- `https://www.50pros.com/support/getting-listed` — "How to create your
  agency profile and get approved." Official onboarding: "Select the Agency
  role during registration and use your work email address (free email
  providers like Gmail or Yahoo are not accepted for agency accounts). After
  creating your account, you'll be guided through a short onboarding wizard:
  enter your agency name and website, select your service categories, set
  your primary location, write an about description, and review your
  details. Once submitted, your profile enters our review queue and will be
  activated upon approval."
- The same page documents the approval process: "After you complete
  onboarding, your profile is placed in 'pending' status for review. Our
  team checks for completeness, legitimacy, and adherence to our content
  guidelines. Once approved, your agency becomes active and visible in the
  directory. To fully unlock your profile (including direct contact info
  visible to clients), you can pay the one-time $350 verification fee from
  the Billing section of your dashboard. Verification is optional but
  recommended — it adds a Verified badge and lets clients see your website,
  email, and phone number."
- The onboarding wizard's eleven steps (verbatim field list): "(1) Agency

  submission."
- Profile completeness: "Your completeness score is calculated from 14

## Never on the profile

- Client names, logos, case studies with client outcomes, testimonials,
  "as seen at", or any client work.
- A base city, office address, or any location the site does not state.
- A phone number, year founded, hourly rate, minimum project size,
  social-profile URLs, or certifications the site does not publish.
- Revenue, ranking, ROAS, conversion, booked-call, or sales-volume results
  or guarantees — including words like "guaranteed growth" or "rankings".
- The retired self-serve Agent Desk as the current product (it is legacy,
  not the current offer; llms.txt "Legacy Self-Serve Agent Desk").
- 50Pros' Verified badge, Featured placement, rankings, "reviews",
  ratings, or any platform statistic — the profile itself must not claim
  results, and no paid upgrade may be purchased without Nish's explicit
  decision.
- Invented case studies, team members, engagement models, or packages.

## Manual submission runbook (Nish, human only)

1. First check that TinyStudio is not already listed: 50Pros may have
   auto-seeded a profile ("Can I claim a profile that already exists on
   50Pros?"). If a profile already exists under a different name form
   (including the legacy Agent Desk), claim it via the claim-invitation
   email or `hello+agencies@50pros.com` and correct it rather than creating
   a duplicate.
2. Open `https://www.50pros.com/auth/signup?role=agency` in a normal
   browser session and register with the Agency role using a work email
   (free providers like Gmail/Yahoo are not accepted). If TinyStudio has no
   work-email domain, contact `hello+agencies@50pros.com` per the venue's
   own guidance before abandoning.
3. Complete the onboarding wizard step by step, filling the fields from the
   table above verbatim where the form accepts the text. Leave empty any
   field the table does not cover, especially: phone, location, year
   founded, hourly rate, minimum project size, social links, certifications.
4. For Portfolio/case studies, use only truthful specimen / proof-of-method
   references (the public `/specimen` page and the `/audit` AI-search
   evidence panel). Do not invent client outcomes. If the wizard blocks
   submission without client case studies, record the rejection as the
   receipt — that is a legitimate truthful outcome per the packet.
5. Review against the "Never on the profile" list before submitting.
6. Submit. Expect the official flow: "pending" status, review "for
   completeness, legitimacy, and adherence to our content guidelines", then
   active and visible in the directory upon approval.
7. Do NOT purchase the $350 Verified upgrade, Featured, or any paid
   placement without Nish's explicit decision.
8. Capture the receipt: the real 50Pros profile URL once published, the
   pending-review state, or the rejection response if review fails. Record
   it in the receipt block below and in the growth-loop packet.

No agent creates the account, fills the form, or submits. No unattended
submission.

## Reject conditions (stop, do not submit, report)

Per the packet "reject if" clause:

- 50Pros requires a city / location, phone number, year founded, hourly
  rate, minimum project size, team size, or named clients that are not on
  the live contract.
- 50Pros requires a paid plan (Verified, Featured, or sponsorship) before a
  basic profile is possible — the base listing is free, so this should not
  happen; if it does, stop.
- 50Pros requires client case studies with client outcomes that TinyStudio
  truthfully cannot provide, and no specimen/proof-of-method alternative is
  accepted — record the rejection as the receipt and stop.
- The live offer cannot be represented without inventing location, clients,
  outcomes, an hourly rate, or a project size.
- No service category in 50Pros' taxonomy fits the offer without
  overclaiming.

In any of these cases the truthful action is to record the blocker in this
receipt block and the growth-loop packet, and stop — silence or invention is
not an outcome.

## Rollback

If 50Pros publishes unsupported or stale claims (wrong product, invented
location, outcome claims, or the retired Agent Desk as current), request
correction or removal manually through the dashboard or
`hello+agencies@50pros.com`, then update the receipt block and the
growth-loop packet with what was requested and when.
