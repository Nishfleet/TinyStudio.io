## Why this handoff exists

The research-desk run on 2026-08-14 verified GoodFirms' official listing
guidance and this lane re-verified every page live on 2026-08-15:

- `https://www.goodfirms.co/get-listed` offers a free plan — "$0 / forever",
  "No card required · 23% acceptance rate" — with "4-Step Research Verification

  sign-in/sign-up flow.

  ("How can my business get listed on Goodfirms?") documents the flow: "Simply
  head over to our Get Listed page. Fill in your necessary company details.
  Choose the appropriate Service Categories for your business (can't be edited
  later.) Submit the form and await approval."
- `https://help.goodfirms.co/is-it-free-to-get-listed-on-goodfirms/` states:
  "Yes! Getting listed on Goodfirms is absolutely free. Fill out the form from
  our Get Listed page and await activation."
- `https://help.goodfirms.co/how-long-does-it-take-for-a-business-listing-to-be-approved-on-goodfirms/`
  states: "You can expect your business listing to be approved within 2-3
  business days after registration."
- `https://help.goodfirms.co/what-information-and-details-should-i-provide-when-submitting-my-business-for-listing/`
  ("What information and details should I provide when submitting my business
  for listing?") lists the collected fields: "Business name, Company website,
  Business tagline, Year founded, Number of employees, Business email address,
  Phone number, Social Profiles (Optional), Certification (if any), Company
  Summary, Cover image for company profile, List of key clients (optional)",
  plus "Location Specific Information" ("Your business' primary and secondary
  location") and "Service, Client and Industry information" (skills, industry,
  clients, niches).
- `https://help.goodfirms.co/why-does-my-company-show-up-in-company-lists-if-i-havent-registered-on-goodfirms/`

  company can take control of such a profile via "Claim your profile".
- `https://www.goodfirms.co/terms-of-use` prohibits, under "Violate our

## Never on the profile

- Client names, logos, case studies, testimonials, "as seen at", or any client
  work.
- A base city, office address, or any location the site does not state.
- A phone number, year founded, social-profile URLs, or certifications the site
  does not publish.
- Revenue, ranking, ROAS, conversion, booked-call, or sales-volume results or
  guarantees — including words like "guaranteed growth" or "rankings".
- An invented hourly rate, project size, employee count (beyond "1", the
  site's own statement), or award.
- The retired self-serve Agent Desk as the current product (it is legacy, not
  the current offer; llms.txt "Legacy Self-Serve Agent Desk").
- GoodFirms' Leader's Matrix ranking, AI-visibility metrics, "reviews",
  ratings, or any platform statistic — the profile itself must not claim
  results.

## Manual submission runbook (Nish, human only)

1. First check that TinyStudio is not already listed: GoodFirms may auto-list a
   company ("Why does my company show up in company lists if I haven't
   registered?"); if a profile already exists under a different name form
   (including the legacy Agent Desk), claim it via "Claim your profile" and
   correct it rather than creating a duplicate.
2. Open `https://www.goodfirms.co/get-listed` in a normal browser session and
   use the free listing path ("Apply for free listing").
3. Sign in / create the vendor account with a human (Nish-owned) email or the
   LinkedIn/Google option. No agent creates the account or fills the form.
4. Fill the fields from the table above, verbatim where the form accepts the
   text. Leave empty any field the table does not cover, especially: phone,
   location, year founded, social profiles, certifications, key clients.
5. Review against the "Never on the profile" list before submitting.
6. Submit. Expect the official flow: research-team verification ("4-Step
   Research Verification by the Goodfirms research team") and approval
   "within 2-3 business days after registration".
7. Capture the receipt: the real GoodFirms profile URL once published, or the
   rejection response if review fails. Record it in the receipt block below
   and in the growth-loop packet.

No agent creates the account, fills the form, or submits. No unattended
submission.

## Reject conditions (stop, do not submit, report)

Per the packet "reject if" clause:

- GoodFirms requires a city / location, phone number, year founded, employee
  count, portfolio, or named clients that are not on the live contract.
- GoodFirms requires a paid plan (PRO or sponsorship) before a basic profile
  is possible — the get-listed Free plan is "$0 / forever, No card required",
  so this should not happen; if it does, stop.
- The live offer cannot be represented without inventing location, clients,
  outcomes, an hourly rate, or a project size.
- No service category in GoodFirms' taxonomy fits the offer without
  overclaiming.

In any of these cases the truthful action is to record the blocker in this
receipt block and the growth-loop packet, and stop — silence or invention is
not an outcome.

## Rollback

If GoodFirms publishes unsupported or stale claims (wrong product, invented
location, outcome claims, or the retired Agent Desk as current), request
correction or removal manually (profile edit or `listing@goodfirms.com`), then
update the receipt block and the growth-loop packet with what was requested
and when.

- The search baseline is not proof that no profile exists; GoodFirms may

- GoodFirms' form fields, service-category taxonomy, and review flow may

  rather than this table verbatim.
- Nothing here predicts publication, visibility, traffic, leads, or revenue.
