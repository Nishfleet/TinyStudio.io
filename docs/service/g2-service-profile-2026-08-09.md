## Why this handoff exists

The research-desk run on 2026-08-09 verified G2's official listing surfaces:

- `https://sell.g2.com/create-a-profile` describes the free request flow: a product
  or service is submitted through a request form, is "conditionally approved", the
  G2 research team "verifies your product or service — in about 3-5 business days —
  before placing it in the right category", the profile then "is live on our site
  and ready to be claimed", and a claim request is "reviewed ... for final approval
  within 1-3 business days". The page also states "you can claim your profile for
  free". The page's "Add your product/service" link opens the request form at
  `https://www.g2.com/products/new`.
- `https://www.g2digitalmarkets.com/listing-guidelines` (Profile Guidelines, "Last
  updated on May 4, 2026") sets the inclusion criteria: the offer "must be publicly
  available, with a call to action that highlights an offer on sale to the public",
  G2 does "not list custom-made/bespoke software", and the listing "must be listed
  under the product or service name on the Vendor's website". Copy rules include: a
  unique, true product overview; the product or service name matching the vendor's
  website; no first-person writing, no calls to action, and no phone, email, fax, or
  URL inside the description; no superlatives or comparative language; no suffixes
  in the company name (no Inc, Corporation, LLC); and "the final copy is subject to
  the discretion of our content team".
- `https://research.g2.com/methodology/research-faq` (G2's official research FAQ)
  states: "Service providers are represented with one profile per vendor", advises
  first checking whether the product or company is "already listed on G2 under a
  different name", and points new profile requests at the request form.

A current web search on 2026-08-09 for `site:g2.com tinystudio.io TinyStudio`
returned no exact TinyStudio/tinystudio.io profile — only unrelated entities
(TinyMCE / Tiny Technologies, TinyURL, Tiiny Host, a TinyOS comparison page). That
search is a baseline, not proof of non-existence. The backlog impact: qualified
buyers on a relevant service-discovery surface have no truthful TinyStudio profile
to evaluate.

Automation disposition (from the packet): ambiguous / manual-only. The official
flow documents a request form and a review, but does not explicitly permit
unattended automation. Absence of permission is not permission: no bot, no
scraping, no account creation by any agent, no unattended submission.

## Never on the profile

- Client names, logos, case studies, testimonials, "as seen at", or any client work.
- A base city or office address.
- Revenue, ranking, ROAS, conversion, booked-call, or sales-volume results or
  guarantees — including words like "guaranteed growth" or "rankings".
- An invented hourly rate, project size, employee count, or award.
- The retired self-serve Agent Desk as the current product (it is legacy, not the
  current offer; llms.txt "Legacy Self-Serve Agent Desk").
- URLs, email addresses, phone numbers, or calls to action inside the description
  text (G2 copy rule).
- First-person writing in the description (G2 copy rule: no "we", "our", "us").

## Manual submission runbook (Nish, human only)

1. First check that TinyStudio is not already listed: G2's FAQ advises confirming
   the product or company is not "already listed on G2 under a different name"
   (service providers get one profile per vendor). The 2026-08-09 baseline search
   found no profile; if one appears under a different name form (including the
   legacy Agent Desk), do not create a duplicate — contact G2 support to update it.
2. Open `https://sell.g2.com/create-a-profile` in a normal browser session, or the
   request form it links to (`https://www.g2.com/products/new`).
3. Fill the request-form fields from the table above. Leave empty any field the
   table does not cover.
4. Submit. Expect the official flow: conditional approval, verification by the G2
   research team "in about 3-5 business days", the profile live and ready to be
   claimed, then a claim request reviewed "for final approval within 1-3 business
   days".
5. Review the published copy against the "Never on the profile" list before and
   after claiming. G2's content team may rewrite the description ("the final copy
   is subject to the discretion of our content team"); the verification step below
   catches any copy that drifted from the live offer.
6. Capture the receipt: the real G2 profile URL once published, or the rejection
   response if review fails. Record it in the receipt block below and in the
   growth-loop packet.

No agent creates the account, fills the form, or submits. No unattended submission.

## Reject conditions (stop, do not submit, report)

Per the packet "reject if" clause:

- G2 classifies the offer as an ineligible bespoke service (its guidelines state
  "we do not list custom-made/bespoke software").
- G2 requires unsupported customer reviews before a truthful profile can exist.
- G2 asks for claims outside the live contract — client names, outcomes, location,
  revenue, rankings, or any figure the site does not state.
- The live offer cannot be represented without inventing location, clients,
  outcomes, an hourly rate, or a project size.

In any of these cases the truthful action is to record the blocker in this receipt
block and the growth-loop packet, and stop — silence or invention is not an outcome.

## Rollback

If G2 publishes unsupported or stale claims (wrong product, invented location,
outcome claims, or the retired Agent Desk as current), request correction or
removal manually through the claimed profile, then update the receipt block and the
growth-loop packet with what was requested and when.
